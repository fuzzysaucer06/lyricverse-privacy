# LYRICVERSE Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

LYRICVERSE is a browser extension that displays synchronized lyrics while users listen to YouTube Music.

## Information We Access

LYRICVERSE accesses the currently playing song's title, artist, and playback position on YouTube Music to identify songs and synchronize lyrics.

## Third-Party Lyrics Service

LYRICVERSE uses the LRCLIB API to retrieve lyrics.

Song-identifying information, such as the track title and artist, is sent to LRCLIB to locate matching lyrics. LRCLIB may receive standard network information, including the IP address associated with the request.

LRCLIB is an independent third-party service.

## Local Storage

LYRICVERSE stores preferences and may cache lyrics locally using Chrome extension storage.

Stored settings may include display colors, font sizes, synchronization adjustments, and floating-window positions.

## Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

LYRICVERSE does not require users to create an account.

LYRICVERSE does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or precise location data.

We do not sell personal information or use collected information for advertising.

## Data Sharing

Song-identifying information is shared with LRCLIB solely to retrieve lyrics.

We do not intentionally transmit users' browsing history, passwords, or personal communications.

## Data Retention

Preferences and cached lyrics remain in local browser storage until cleared by the user or removed through extension uninstallation, subject to Chrome's storage behavior.

## Security

LYRICVERSE uses HTTPS when requesting lyrics from LRCLIB.

## Children's Privacy

LYRICVERSE is not specifically designed for children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when LYRICVERSE's functionality or data practices change.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the LYRICVERSE publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing.

## Disclaimer

LYRICVERSE is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
